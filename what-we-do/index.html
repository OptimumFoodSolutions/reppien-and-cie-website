What we do

Capital allocation is the key to value creation

Expert wealth advisory for generational success

As experienced advisors, Reppien & Cie assist high-net-worth families and individuals in navigating complex financial challenges. Our mission is simple: to secure your wealth for generations to come through tailored advice and lasting relationships.

We are experts in value investing, which involves looking for undervalued companies with strong fundamentals and holding onto them for the long term, with deep knowledge of selected American and European companies and thus securities, including stocks and preferred stocks. We view ourselves as long-term owners of companies and not as owners of securities to be traded frequently.

A focused approach to securities

We are focused primarily on European and American listed securities, i.e. common stocks, preferred stocks, and corporate bonds. We analyze and monitor a select number of securities based on fine-tuned screening criteria and models – we are experts on the selected companies, we monitor and invest in with deep knowledge of their businesses.

Tailored portfolios

Based on a proven acumen, we are developing tailormade investment portfolios to high net worth individuals and family offices and in parallel we monitor these portfolios and provide regular updates to our clients. Reppien & Cie will not and does not have access to client cash – all trades are handled and managed by the clients themselves.

We are also managing our own fund, the “Reppien & Cie Select Value Fund” which was established in 2025. The fund is actively managed. Clients can particpate by buying shares in the fund – click here for more information.

M&A activities and advising

Reppien & Cie is also actively engaged in advising and assisting family offices in transactions related to M&A activities, i.e., divestments and acquistions of companies and business units, including valuation, development of information memorandums, dialogue with potantial buyers and sellers, commercial due diligence, and shapening and negotiating deals. Our partners have substantial experience in M&A work.

Family office consulting and formation

We are increasingly asked to advise wealthy individuals and families on how to design and develop a family office that has: 1) the right governance structure, 2) articulated investment principles and approach, 3) a clear investment strategy, 4) and importantly the right competencies in place. With the purpose of preserving and creating wealth for existing and future generations.

Reppien & Cie have profound knowledge of capital allocation – the key to wealth management and value creation.
